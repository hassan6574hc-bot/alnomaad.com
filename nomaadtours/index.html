Why Nomaad Tours

Most Madinah tours are the same.
This isn't one of them.

There are hundreds of coaches circling the same sites with the same stories. This is something different.

01
Your group only. No strangers.
You will not share a vehicle, a guide, or an experience with anyone outside your group. Private by design — not as an upgrade, but as a minimum.
02
Narrated from Qur'an and sunnah. Nothing else.
Every site explained through authentic sources only. No folklore. No fabrications. You leave knowing what is real — a distinction rarely made in group tours.
03
Led by a resident — not a visitor.
Hassan is based in Madinah. He knows the city not as a visitor passing through, but as someone who lives and breathes it day to day. The Souk, the streets, the seasons — all of it is part of daily life.
04
Unhurried. No clock watching.
There is no group to keep up with and no next coach to catch. You stay as long as you need at every site. The experience moves at your pace, not a timetable.
05
Umrah done right — not rushed.
The 3-Day package includes a full seminar before Makkah — every step of tawaf, sa'i, and the rituals explained so nothing is left uncertain. Then guided personally, into Fajr.
06
A price that rewards the group.
Every additional person reduces the per-person cost. Families and larger groups are actively rewarded — not penalised — for being together.
