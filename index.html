FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Still have something specific? The Discovery Call is the fastest way to get a real answer.

01 Does the Discovery Call fee count toward a package?

Yes. If you go on to book any package or bundle priced over £150 — the Hijrah Compass, the Madinah Blueprint, the Full Landing, or either Property Bundle — your Discovery Call fee is credited in full. You pay for the call once, not twice.

02 Is this only for Madinah, or do you cover other cities?

Al-Nomaad is built specifically for Madinah — it is where the consultancy is permanently based, and what makes on-ground services possible. Consultations can cover other cities for context, but property work and practical services are Madinah-specific.

03 I'm not sure which package is right for me.

Start with the Discovery Call. You will get an honest recommendation on which package — or individual service — fits your situation. If none of them are right yet, that will be said plainly. There is no pressure to commit to anything on the call.

04 What does the property inspection actually cover?

40+ checkpoints, photos included, written report delivered within 48 hours of the visit. The inspection covers structural condition, Haram proximity timed on foot, noise levels at different times of day, landlord assessment, and everything you need to make a confident decision from abroad.

05 I'm a sister planning to move. Is this set up for that?

Al-Nomaad works with sisters travelling with a mahram. Community connections include families and sisters already settled in Madinah. Safety is factored into every neighbourhood and property assessment — including building access, proximity to facilities, and neighbour profile.

06 Can I get Iqama help without taking Tier Three?

Iqama facilitation is included in Tier Three. The Iqama route can be discussed in any consultation — including the Discovery Call — so you understand the process and what it involves before committing to a package.
